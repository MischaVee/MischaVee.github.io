Ideas in motion

Longer-running projects — some shot, some sketched, some still just a question I keep returning to. Each one gets its own page as it develops; for now, here's where they stand.

Project 01

What People Find Attractive

Photo series — in progress

A portrait series built around a single question asked of every subject: what do you find attractive, and in whom or what did you first notice it? The photographs try to hold both things at once — the subject as they are, and some trace of what they're drawn to, whether that shows up as a prop, a pose, a gesture, or just how they choose to be looked at.

The interest isn't in a single definition of attraction but in how personal and specific it turns out to be once you actually ask — replace this with your own framing as the series develops.

Project 02

My Life in Chapters of Colors

Visual essay — ongoing

An autobiographical project organized by color instead of by year — each chapter of life assigned the color that dominates the memory of it, then built out visually: images, objects, palettes pulled from that period. The chapter spine running down the edge of this site is a direct preview of the idea.

Swap in your actual chapters here as you define them — what each color period covers, roughly when it ran, and what it looked like.

Project 03

Visual Studies of Musicians

Thematic analysis — ongoing

A close reading of the visual language specific musicians build around their music — cover art, stage design, color, wardrobe, typography — and what that imagery is doing that the music alone doesn't. Each entry treats an artist's visual world as seriously as their sound.

Start this with one or two artists you already have strong opinions about, and let the format prove itself before scaling it up.
